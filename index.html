Keep Assets Safe — Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026

1. At a glance

Where helps you remember where your things are kept. Its personal collection is stored on your Android device, not in a developer-managed cloud collection. The app includes optional app lock, photos, lending records, and encrypted backups. A separate Google Play subscription and integrity workflow may process limited purchase-verification information, as described below.

2. Information kept on your device

Item names, locations, category labels, tags, notes or clues, spaces, item photos, lending information, and related history are saved locally. Where uses Android Keystore-backed protection for its local collection. Your app PIN and exported-backup password are not sent to our verification service. You can remove entries in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local records, subject to any backups you previously exported.

3. Photos and backups

Photo selection is initiated by you through Android's system picker or camera workflow. Where does not upload your photos for advertising or analytics. You can manually create an encrypted backup and choose where Android saves it; that destination may be local storage or a provider you select. Where does not automatically sync backups to our servers. You control separately exported backup files and their deletion.

4. Google Play subscriptions and verification

Where offers Free and Pro functionality. When a Pro purchase or restore is available, Google Play processes payment. To validate Pro entitlements and prevent tampering, the app's verification component may send the purchase token, product identifier, Google Play Integrity token, app package identifier and a randomly generated installation binding over HTTPS to a developer verification service. That service checks entitlement validity and responds with a signed access grant. It does not need or receive your saved item collection, photos, app PIN or backup password.

Google handles purchase and integrity processing under its own applicable policies. Availability of paid purchases depends on the Play release and configured verification service. Verification records may be retained as needed to administer subscriptions, prevent abuse, investigate fraud and comply with applicable obligations, after which they are deleted or de-identified as appropriate.

5. Ads, analytics, sharing and access

Where does not contain an advertising SDK and does not sell personal data to advertisers. The app does not upload your local item collection for developer analytics. App lock can use a PIN you choose or Android biometric authentication; Where does not receive your biometric template. Internet access is used for optional Google Play billing and integrity workflows, not for reading or uploading your personal inventory.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Delete individual records in the app, or clear the app's storage to remove the local collection.
  • Remove separately exported backups in the destination where you saved them.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.
  • For inquiries about subscription verification records, contact the developer using the email below.

7. Children and changes

Where is a general-purpose asset organizer intended for adults and is not designed for children. We may revise this notice as the app's features change, and will update the effective date above.
